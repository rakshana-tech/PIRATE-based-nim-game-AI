📜 RULES OF PIRATE'S NIM

Study the ancient corsair code before entering the treasure chamber.

1

Multiple Heaps of Treasure

The game board contains multiple distinct heaps of shiny pirate gold coins.

2

One Heap Per Turn

On your turn, you can select only ONE heap from which to take coins.

3

Take Any Positive Amount

You can remove anywhere from 1 coin up to all coins in that selected heap.

4

No Multi-Heap Splitting

A player cannot split their move across multiple heaps in a single turn.

5

Alternating Turns

The human captain and AI opponent alternate turns with no skipping allowed.

6

Normal Play Victory

The player who takes the very last coin from the board wins the entire pirate treasure!
